_Need nerves of tungsten._

I am a graduate student in mathematics at **Jilin University**. I am interested in numerical inverse problems, wave scattering, and stochastic dynamics.

My work is a mixture of mathematical study, computational experiments, and software development. Some projects began as coursework or collaborations; others began simply because I wanted to try something. Not all of them are finished.

I am now working to turn selected experiments into **reproducible, documented works**.

### Current directions

- **Acoustic scattering and inverse problems.** Computational benchmarks for Helmholtz scattering, with numerical solvers and reconstruction problems as future steps.
- **Stochastic dynamics and market inference.** A developing research direction concerned with inferring hidden parameters and mechanisms from observations. No predictive performance is claimed.
