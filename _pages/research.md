My mathematical work currently focuses on numerical inverse problems. I am also developing a separate line of questions about stochastic systems and financial markets.

## Acoustic scattering and inverse problems

The starting point is the two-dimensional Helmholtz equation and scattering by a sound-soft obstacle.

My initial computational study uses an **analytical Fourier–Hankel series solution** for a circular obstacle as a benchmark. It is not yet an independent FEM/BEM solver or an inverse-reconstruction result.

The planned progression is analytical benchmarks, independent numerical forward solvers, inverse reconstruction, and error/stability analysis. Each completed stage will be documented separately.

## Stochastic dynamics and financial inverse problems

I am interested in whether observable time series and market microstructure data can identify parameters or latent features of stochastic models. This is a proposed direction, not a claim of a working trading strategy.

Related earlier exercises include Langevin dynamics, numerical SDE experiments, and parameter-estimation ideas developed during a 2025 exchange at the University of Alberta.

## Working principles

- Preserve the distinction between an experiment, a validated result, and an open question.
- Publish code, computational settings, tests, and limitations when possible.
- Maintain stable releases while continuing to explore new ideas.
